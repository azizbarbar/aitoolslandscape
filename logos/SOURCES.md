# Logo sources

PNG files were copied from the user-supplied How Machines Think Presentation/_source/assets/logo folder. Only matched products are displayed; no substitute logo is used for a renamed product without verified identity.

Additional monochrome SVGs: @lobehub/icons-static-svg version 1.95.1, downloaded from npm on October 15, 2026 with package scripts disabled. Selected files: copilot.svg, deepseek.svg, grok.svg, metaai.svg, mistral.svg. Copilot and Meta AI are also copied to their roster IDs. Logo rights remain with their respective owners.

## October 15 expansion

All profiles now have a local mark. `research/logo-acquisition.json` records the exact public icon URLs downloaded from the official websites. Added official icons include Elicit, Consensus, ResearchRabbit, Magnific, Vizcom, CLO, Emergent, Bolt, Blotato, Higgsfield, Weave, OpenArt and Google Flow. Canva's icon came from `https://static.canva.com/static/images/favicon-1.ico`.

ComfyUI, FLORA, Lovart, fal and Replicate use monochrome SVGs from the same LobeHub package. Krea and Adobe Firefly platform profiles reuse their existing supplied marks.

Where official downloads were unavailable, Scite, Gamma, NewArc, Leonardo and LTX use locally drawn typographic brand treatments: an initial, letterform or abbreviated wordmark based on the product name. These are approximate identification marks, not official logo artwork. This follows the user's express permission to draw from branding. They are kept black to match the atlas. Brand rights remain with their owners.
