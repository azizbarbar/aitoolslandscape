# Visual plan

The object is a delegation atlas for a design workshop. The cloud itself is the principal visual event. Every surrounding element either names a tool, explains an axis, or changes a comparison.

Palette: paper #FFFFFF, ink #111111, secondary #6B6B6B, rule #D9D9D9, collaborator #694191, curator #0365AF. Purple and blue appear primarily as extremely light half-plane washes; ink carries the clouds. Green #007145 is reserved for the quiet active-state indicator.

Typography: local VT323 at a large editorial scale for the title, axes, band titles and selected tool names. Local IBM Plex Mono for navigation, small explanatory copy, coordinates and evidence. No decorative type families.

Layout: a generous title with a restrained instructional sentence; a four-part category rail; a tool list at left, a large square map centered, and a quiet selected-tool/band-meaning rail at right. The map is the dominant area. At laptop heights the rails remain scrollable without shrinking the map labels into tiny text. At phone widths categories form a two-by-two selector, tools become a short scrollable list, and details sit below the map.

Review against the brief: hairline rules and monospace are intentional because they belong to the supplied atlas direction. Avoid turning the layout into a generic newspaper or a dashboard: no stacked cards, metric tiles, marketing hero, gradients, shadows, or unrelated labels. Category numbering serves the actual keyboard shortcuts. All-caps is limited to the prescribed map vocabulary.

Cloud treatment: clean dark contour with a clipped, sparse typographic field; comparison contour dashed and interior crosses. Keep the field quiet enough to read the quadrant names. The default dot is a crosshair, not a badge. Selection animates only the affected cloud; the entire interface does not slide around.

Interaction: click a second tool to compare; when two are active, click a third to replace the second. Selected tools can be removed from either list or readout. Clear resets selections, not category. Category change carries a morph of the first cloud into that category's first active tool. Selection details are collapsed by default.
