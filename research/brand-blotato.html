FAQ

Real questions. Straight answers.

01 How does Blotato work?
You bring a topic. Blotato runs it through proven templates and a model fine-tuned on viral posts, then formats the output for each platform you publish to. Schedule it from inside the app, or pipe it through the API into your own automation.
02 Who is Blotato for?
Solopreneurs, creators, small business owners, and small agencies who want to grow a brand or audience without hiring a content team. Especially good if you're running an 8-in-1 setup today (writer, scheduler, design tool, video tool, automation, etc.) and want one bill instead of six.
03 Who is Blotato NOT for?
Long-form video clipping. UGC ad farms. SEO-only operators chasing keyword rankings. People who want to spam low-effort AI content without adding their own perspective. We're picky on purpose.
04 Will scheduling posts hurt my reach?
No. The founder grew 0 → 170k Instagram on 100% scheduled posts, and the same on LinkedIn. Modern platforms are interest-based, not follower-based - what matters is whether the content is good.
05 Do I need to install anything?
No. Blotato is a web app. The API and MCP server are optional, for users wiring it into Claude, n8n, Make, or their own agents.
06 Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Start with a free week. If it's not earning back the subscription, walk away - no clawback, no calls.
